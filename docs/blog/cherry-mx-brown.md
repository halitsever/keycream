# Cherry MX Brown sound and feel, explained

<p class="post-meta">Halit Sever · 28 November 2026</p>

The Cherry MX Brown is the middle ground of Cherry's classic lineup: it has a bump you can feel, like a clicky switch, but no click you can hear, so it sounds closer to a linear switch. This post covers what Cherry says about it, how it sits between Red and Blue, and a recording of it you can play here.

## Cherry's specs

From Cherry's product page for the current MX2A Brown, checked on 28 November 2026:

| | Cherry MX Brown |
| --- | --- |
| Type | tactile, no click |
| Operating force | 55 cN in the page highlights |
| Actuation force | 45 cN in the properties table |
| Initial / final force | 30 cN / 80 cN |
| Pre-travel | 2.0 mm |
| Total travel | 4.0 mm |

The page lists two force figures without explaining them. Cherry's datasheet for the switch describes 45 cN as the actuation force and 55 cN as the pressure point, the top of the tactile bump. KeyCream's pack doesn't say which generation of MX Brown was recorded, so these numbers describe the switch, not the recording.

## How the bump feels

A tactile switch pushes back a little more at one point in the stroke, then gives way. On the Brown that bump is light: Cherry calls it "focused and noticeable."

## Brown next to Red and Blue

| | Red | Brown | Blue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Type | linear | tactile | tactile and clicky |
| Operating force (highlights) | 45 cN | 55 cN | 60 cN |
| Pre-travel | 2.0 mm | 2.0 mm | 2.2 mm |
| Click you can hear | no | no | yes |

All from Cherry's MX2A product pages. The Blue's page also lists 50 cN in its properties table. [Cherry MX Red vs Brown vs Blue](/blog/cherry-mx-red-vs-brown-vs-blue) puts all three recordings side by side.

## What a Brown sounds like

Without a click, a Brown's sound is mostly the stem hitting the bottom and springing back, the same sources as a linear switch. Compared with the Blue recording, the difference is the missing click on every press.

## Cherry MX Brown sounds on a Mac

[KeyCream](/) plays this recording for every key you press, from the Mac menu bar. Choose **Cherry MX Brown** under **Sound Pack**. It is free and open source, runs on macOS 14 or later, and only reads which key went down. You get the sound, not the feel: the keys on your Mac stay the same.
