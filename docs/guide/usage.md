# Using KeyCream

KeyCream has no window. Everything happens from its icon in the menu bar, a small keycap with cream dripping from it.

## The menu

| Item | What it does | Starts as |
|---|---|---|
| **Sounds On** | Turns key sounds on or off without quitting the app. | On |
| **Volume** | Low, Medium or High. | High |
| **Sound Pack** | Chooses which keyboard you hear. The new pack plays right away. | NovelKeys Cream |
| **Open at Login** | Starts KeyCream when you log in to your Mac. | On |
| **Language** | **System Language** follows your Mac. Choose a language to use that one instead. | System Language |
| **Quit** | Closes KeyCream until you open it again. Shortcut: <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Q</kbd> while the menu is open. | |

Every choice is saved as soon as you make it and is still there after you restart your Mac.

## What makes a sound

- **A key going down.** Releasing a key is silent.
- **Modifier keys** such as <kbd>shift</kbd>, <kbd>command</kbd>, <kbd>option</kbd> and <kbd>control</kbd> when you press them. Left and right keys are told apart.
- **Caps Lock**, each time you press it.

Holding a key down plays one sound. The repeated characters macOS types while you hold it stay silent.

## Volume

The menu volume works on top of your Mac's volume. On **High**, KeyCream plays each sound at the level the pack was leveled to. **Medium** and **Low** play it at 60% and 30% of that.

Every pack is leveled when it loads, so switching packs doesn't make your keyboard suddenly louder or quieter.

## Languages

KeyCream is available in English, Türkçe, Deutsch, Français, Español, Português, Русский, 日本語, 한국어, 简体中文, العربية and हिन्दी.

With **System Language** it uses the first language in **System Settings → General → Language & Region** that it supports, and Turkish if none of them is. You can also set KeyCream's language there, under **Applications**, and the menu shows the same choice.
