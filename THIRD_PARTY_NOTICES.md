# Third-party sound packs

KeyCream ships Mechvibes-format sound packs from the [Mechvibes](https://github.com/hainguyents13/mechvibes)
project. Each pack folder in `Resources/SoundPacks/` carries the license as `LICENSE.txt`.

| Pack folder | Origin | License |
|---|---|---|
| NovelKeys-cream | Mechvibes `src/audio/nk-cream`, "NK Cream (original by Ryan)" | MIT, Copyright (c) 2021 Hai Nguyen |
| CherryMX-black-pbt | Mechvibes `src/audio/cherrymx-black-pbt` | MIT, Copyright (c) 2021 Hai Nguyen |
| CherryMX-blue-pbt | Mechvibes `src/audio/cherrymx-blue-pbt` | MIT, Copyright (c) 2021 Hai Nguyen |
| CherryMX-brown-pbt | Mechvibes `src/audio/cherrymx-brown-pbt` | MIT, Copyright (c) 2021 Hai Nguyen |
| CherryMX-red-pbt | Mechvibes `src/audio/cherrymx-red-pbt` | MIT, Copyright (c) 2021 Hai Nguyen |
| Everglide-crystal-purple | Mechvibes `src/audio/eg-crystal-purple` | MIT, Copyright (c) 2021 Hai Nguyen |
| Everglide-oreo | Mechvibes `src/audio/eg-oreo` | MIT, Copyright (c) 2021 Hai Nguyen |

The single-file packs (Cherry MX, Everglide) were converted from OGG to WAV. Their key
definitions are unchanged.

Switch names (NovelKeys, Cherry MX, Everglide) are trademarks of their owners and only
describe which switch a recording is of.
